# Positioning v3 — The Distilled Value Proposition

## The one sentence (locked, used verbatim)

**Real medicine, in a place worth coming back to — among peers who already understand.**

This is the line. It appears verbatim in three places:
1. Hero subhead (under the H1).
2. Footer invariants rail (replaces the three-column "30-min callback / 24–72hr admission / one principal" trio as the *thesis* line above the rail).
3. Homepage `<meta name="description">` and `og:description`.

## Why this sentence

It does three jobs in nineteen words:
- **"Real medicine"** — establishes clinical seriousness without using "Swiss," "board-certified," or any framework word. The proof shows up in the body; the headline doesn't have to argue.
- **"a place worth coming back to"** — restorative pleasure as a *return invitation*, not as indulgence. Implies success (you'd come back), implies the place itself matters, implies the work is finite (you leave).
- **"among peers who already understand"** — peer belonging as the discretion mechanism. Privacy is implicit; the room is composed of equals, so explanation is unnecessary.

## The three proofs (one line each, used as Hero H1 trio + meta)

1. **Clinical depth.** Board-certified leadership. 4:1 staff-to-guest. One family in residence.
2. **Peer belonging.** The chair you sit in has been sat in by a founder, a partner, a justice. You will not be the first.
3. **Restorative pleasure.** Trout streams. Horses. Long-table dinners. The work is real. The week is yours.

## What v3 changes from v2

- The line "Care worth coming to. Among peers who quietly understand." retires. It served v2 well; v3 tightens it because "care" is softer than the buyer needs and "quietly" was throat-clearing — peers who *already* understand is sharper than peers who quietly understand.
- "Care" → "Real medicine" — the buyer is choosing between a luxury spa with therapy bolted on and *us*. We must say medicine.
- "coming to" → "coming back to" — restorative pleasure as *return*. One word, big lift.
