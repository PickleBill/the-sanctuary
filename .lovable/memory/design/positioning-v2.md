# Positioning v2 — "The Obviously Better Alternative"

## The thesis (one sentence)
The alternative for an HNW executive in crisis is drinking alone in a hotel room and shame-spiraling. Sanctuary Southeast is *obviously* better than that — not because it's the most discreet, but because what happens here is privately, quietly, **enjoyable**. You're with peers who get it. You leave with friends. You laugh. You ride. You eat well. You feel like a person again.

## The three pillars (in priority order)
1. **Clinical depth** — Swiss-standard medicine, board-certified leadership, 4:1 staff-to-guest. (Unchanged.)
2. **Peer belonging** — *new pillar.* You are not the first. The guest before you was a founder, a partner, a surgeon, a justice. They sat in your chair. Privacy is implicit because the room is composed of peers, not strangers.
3. **Restorative pleasure** — *new pillar.* Trout streams. Horses. Long-table dinners. A book by the fire. The *life* you forgot you wanted. Joy as proof of concept, not as indulgence.

Privacy moves from headline to **floor**. It is the precondition that lets the other three things happen, not the product itself.

## Voice — do / don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Warmth, peer-belonging, restorative pleasure | Glamour, party, indulgence, "spa" |
| "Care worth coming to." | "The most discreet retreat in America." |
| "You will eat better here than at home." | "World-class culinary program." |
| "An hour with a horse who doesn't care what you do." | "Equine-assisted therapy modalities." |
| "You will not be the first." | Testimonials, names, faces, before/afters |
| Joy. Quiet competence. Editorial restraint. | Exclamation points. Marketing exuberance. |

## Cascade rules
- Every section eyebrow may now reference the human, not just the asset
- Every long caption ends on a human sentence, not a fact
- Amber discipline unchanged: eyebrow rule + active node + primary CTA only
- Stillness is still the brand; motion serves stillness, not the other way around

## What this thesis unlocks
Two new sections that did not exist in v1.5:
- **You are not the first** (peer cohort) — between Resonance and Amenities
- **A Day Here** (joy proof) — between Amenities and Leadership

These two surfaces are the structural answer to "the site reads like a vault." The vault is still there. It's just no longer the storefront.
