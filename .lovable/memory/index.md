# Sanctuary Southeast — Project Memory

## Core
Positioning v3 — locked: "The room is the medicine. / Care, in the company of peers." (See mem://design/positioning-v3)
v3.3 rule: Show, don't tell. Max one paragraph per section. Imagery & motion are the headline. (See mem://design/show-not-tell-v33)
v3.5 brand: Sanctuary Gold (warm amber) + Ember accent duet. H2 weight 600 desktop / 550 mobile. .text-luxe on photo headlines. Snappy 240ms easings. (See mem://design/v35-vibrancy)
v3.5 AI rule: every AI-composed surface shows <AIPresenceChip /> with 3-line transparency popover. Trust earned through transparency, not hidden tech.
Mobile-first motion: every interactive surface must autoplay on mobile. Hover is a desktop bonus.
Geography: Western North Carolina (Asheville / Tryon / Lake Lure / Highlands corridor) — say "Blue Ridge", not "the Southeast".
Audience is THREE: principal/family, referral professional (interventionist/concierge MD/family-office advisor/attorney), trusted advisor the URL is forwarded to.
Site is brochureware for credibility, NOT a conversion funnel. HNW prospects read deeply then forward to advisors.
Named clinical leadership (MD + Clinical Director) IS the brand — not the building.
Anti-signals: NEVER show celebrity names, pricing, "spa with therapy bolted on" language, testimonials, before/afters. NO wine/glasses/alcohol on a wellness/recovery site. NO golf-resort imagery.
Required artifacts: downloadable PDF clinical summary, /professionals page (with composeReferralNote AI tool), displayed 24/7 admissions line, per-route OG share cards.
Design: sharp 0px radii, navy/ivory/gold/ember palette, amber filament SVG language, easing cubic-bezier(0.32, 0, 0.18, 1) for snap and (0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) for luxe. Typography: Literata single-family (opsz 7..72). NEVER use Inter, Playfair, or any font on the Impeccable reflex-reject list.
Anti-kickback transparency: "We do not pay referral fees." is itself a credibility signal.
HIPAA: NO PHI on the marketing site or in Cloud DB. Clinical data belongs in EMR (Kipu/Sunwave) outside Lovable. Referrer note composer strips identifiers silently.
Impeccable framework vendored at .impeccable/ — use its 18 commands as design lenses; project context locked in .impeccable.md.
v3.7 — Synergy section LEADS with SynergyMap (6 clinical/holistic pairs, swipeable on mobile), WeekRhythm preserved as quiet footer strip below.
v3.7 — Leadership shows THREE clinicians (Medical · Clinical · Family Program). Warm Kinfolk-style portraits. Names placeholder w/ honest footer.
v3.7 — Cohort mobile: name plate ABOVE the SVG, "View the room — 40" chip BELOW opens bottom sheet of all archetypes.
v3.7 — Logo is a double-ridge silhouette + first-light sunrise dot. Hamburger nav is monochrome ivory, no amber middle bar.
