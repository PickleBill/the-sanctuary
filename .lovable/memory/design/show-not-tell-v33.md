# Show, don't tell — v3.3 lock

The room IS the medicine. The product is the *experience of the room* — not a description of it.

## Rules

1. **Maximum one paragraph per section.** If a section needs more than one paragraph to land, split it into two sections or cut copy until it fits.
2. **Imagery, motion, and interaction are the headline.** Words are the support beam.
3. **Adventure AND introspection.** Activation AND restoration. Never one without the other.
4. **No corporate framing language.** "Designed for…", "We provide…", "Our offering…" are forbidden.
5. **Mobile-first motion.** Every interactive surface must autoplay on mobile. Hover is a desktop bonus, never a mobile gate.
6. **Anonymous humans are humans.** Backs of heads, hands, silhouettes — but humans, not buildings.

## What this kills

- DayHere-style solo timelines (already cut in v3.2)
- "About the building" galleries (cut in v3.3 — Gallery → Journey)
- Flat-stack contact forms (cut in v3.3 — ConciergeForm → conversational stepper)
- Static peer lists (cut in v3.2 — PeerCohort → Cohort constellation)

## What this rewards

- Cinemagraphs, auto-Ken-Burns, auto-tour constellations
- Single-image bento grids
- Conversational forms with prose-embedded inputs
- Section-boundary filaments that make the page feel one continuous breath
