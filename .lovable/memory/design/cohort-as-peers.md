---
name: Cohort as peers, not titles
description: All Cohort/Leadership archetypes lead with what people DO or CARE ABOUT, never job titles. The room must feel human, not institutional.
type: constraint
---

**Rule:** Every archetype in `src/lib/cohort/roles.ts` and any leadership/peer-facing copy must read as a sentence about a person — what they DO, what they CARE about, what they're LEARNING — never a job title or institutional credential.

**Wrong (institutional, judgy):**
- "Federal Judge"
- "Three-Star General · retired"
- "Pediatric Oncologist"
- "Surgeon · Mayo"

**Right (peer at the dinner table):**
- "The judge who learned to fish here"
- "The general who keeps bees"
- "The doctor halfway through her second novel"
- "The surgeon who started painting again"

**Why:** The Cohort is meant to feel like recognition (someone like me is here) not surveillance (the AI is auditing me). Title-based archetypes reproduce a perp-walk feel and contradict the brand's quiet-luxury, peer-not-patient ethos.

**Also forbidden:** weight indicators (`[09 / 100]`), dim-fade on inactive nodes, "judging" copy. The matched node uses `--bloom` (dusty rose) as the AI-moment color — never gold/ember which are routine brand tones.
