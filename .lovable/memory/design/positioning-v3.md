# Positioning v3 — Locked

## The line (verbatim)

**The room is the medicine.**
*Care, in the company of peers.*

## Why this order

Every prior pass led with the building, the program, or "discretion." Every
pass therefore felt like a private cottage. v3 puts the **room** (peer
network) in front of the **care** (clinical work). The room is what no other
treatment center can reproduce. The clinic is what makes the room safe.

## Three proofs (used wherever the value prop needs evidence)

1. **The Cohort surface** — anonymous constellation of recent residents.
   The peer network is *visible*, not implied.
2. **Named clinical leadership** — MD + Clinical Director. The room is safe
   because the medicine is real.
3. **One family in residence at a time** — the peer-room is small enough
   that everyone in it is at the same altitude.

## Use the line verbatim in three places

- **Hero headline** — `The room is the medicine.` / `Care, in the company of peers.`
- **Footer invariant line**
- **Meta description** of the homepage

## Voice rules (carry forward from v2)

- Never lead with "private," "discreet," or "luxury."
- Never describe the building before describing the room.
- Plural pronouns ("peers," "the room," "the cohort") beat singular pronouns
  ("you," "the guest").
- Italics carry the human side; roman carries the clinical side.
