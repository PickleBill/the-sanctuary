# Sanctuary Southeast — Design Context

> Project-level context loaded automatically by the Impeccable framework
> before any design skill runs. Edit when product positioning changes.

## Target Audience

THREE concentric audiences (per Strategic Brief §3):

1. **The Principal / Family** — high-functioning C-suite executive (Director,
   VP, Partner) or family member of one. Reads deeply, in private, often at
   night. Will not fill out a form. Forwards the URL to a trusted advisor.
   Net worth $10M+; UHNW $100M+ also represented.
2. **The Referral Professional** — interventionist, concierge MD, addiction
   psychiatrist, family-office advisor, trusts-and-estates attorney, executive
   coach. Reads to assess clinical legitimacy. Looks for credentials, named
   leadership, anti-kickback transparency, outcomes posture.
3. **The Trusted Advisor** — the person the principal forwards the URL to
   (CFO, family lawyer, longtime physician). Often the actual decision-maker.
   Skeptical, time-poor, high pattern recognition for "spa with therapy
   bolted on" red flags.

## Use Cases

- Read 3-5 pages deeply in private
- Forward URL to a trusted advisor
- Download a clinical dossier PDF to share offline
- (Professional only) Submit a referral with one-line non-clinical note
- (Professional only) Verify clinical leadership credentials and accreditations
- Call a 24/7 admissions line answered by a masters-level clinician

## Brand Personality / Tone

Three concrete words: **clinical, restrained, unhurried**.

- NOT "modern" or "elegant" — those are dead categories.
- NOT "wellness/spa" — explicit anti-pattern.
- Closest reference points (physical objects, not websites):
  - The interior typography of a Mayo Clinic referral packet
  - The plate caption font on a vintage botanical engraving
  - The matte cream cardstock of a private bank's introductory letter
  - The hand-set serif of a 19th-century medical journal
- Visual atmosphere: a Maine boathouse at first light, not a Vegas spa lobby.
- Confidence comes from restraint. The site whispers; it does not sell.

## Constraints

- **Tech**: TanStack Start v1 + React 19 + Tailwind v4 + Lovable Cloud
- **Performance**: must render fast on a 4G iPhone in a hospital waiting room
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA minimum across both light and dark themes
- **Compliance**: NO PHI on the marketing site. No celebrity references.
  No pricing. No before/after. No testimonials. (HIPAA + brand integrity.)
- **Audience-driven theme**: light theme primary (read in waiting rooms,
  hospital lounges, late at night on bedside iPads — never a trading desk).
  Dark optional, never default.

## Differentiation

The one unforgettable thing: **named clinical leadership presented like the
masthead of a private medical journal — not the staff page of a resort.**
Everything else (estate photography, amenities, process) is supporting cast
to that masthead.

Secondary signal: **anti-kickback transparency.** "We do not pay referral
fees." printed plainly on /professionals. The honesty itself is the moat.

## Anti-Patterns (HARD bans — do not even propose)

- Celebrity/influencer endorsements
- Before/after imagery
- Pricing or "starting at" language anywhere
- Spa terminology ("pamper", "indulge", "treat yourself")
- Testimonials with full names + faces
- Stock photos of smiling people in robes
- Lifestyle marketing copy ("transform your life", "your best self")
- Generic AI hero patterns: centered headline + gradient blob + 3 feature cards
- Inter, Roboto, Open Sans, system-default fonts
- Playfair Display, Fraunces, Cormorant, DM Serif (Impeccable reflex list)
- Purple gradients, rounded blob shapes, blurred orbs
- "Cards on cards on cards" layouts

## Design System (locked tokens)

- **Geometry**: 0px border radius everywhere. Sharp edges only.
- **Palette** (OKLCH, see src/styles.css):
  - Foundation: deep navy (`oklch(0.18 0.02 250)`) and ivory (`oklch(0.97 0.01 85)`)
  - Single accent: amber filament (`oklch(0.72 0.15 70)`) — used at <10% visual weight
  - Neutrals tinted ~0.005 chroma toward navy hue for cohesion
- **Type pairing**: distinctive editorial serif + a quiet humanist sans
  (specific fonts pending — Playfair is being replaced, see open question)
- **Motion**: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), durations 400-800ms.
  No spring physics. No bounce. No parallax. One slow Ken Burns drift only.
- **SVG language**: thin amber filament lines tying clinical and holistic
  sides of the Synergy Map. Reuse this filament motif for Process timeline
  and any future connectors.

## Voice & UX Writing

- Sentences end. Em-dashes are allowed; exclamation points are not.
- No second-person sales voice ("you'll love…"). Third-person clinical or
  first-person plural restraint ("We have found…", "Guests in residence…").
- Numbers spelled out below ten. Roman numerals for ordered ledgers.
- CTAs are quiet imperatives: *Request the Clinical Dossier*, not *Get Yours Now*.
